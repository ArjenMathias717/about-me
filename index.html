About Rebecca

Rebecca Martins is a Brazilian transgender girl, googologist, and creator of large-number systems and mathematical notations. She is particularly interested in fast-growing hierarchies, ordinal notation, enormous numbers, Knuth's up-arrow notation, and fictional googology.

Her work focuses on exploring the behavior and representation of extremely large numbers through original systems, functions, notations, and mathematical concepts. Her projects range from fast-growing functions and ordinal structures to custom operators and array-based notation.

History in Googology

Rebecca discovered googology through the online googology community, where she became interested in increasingly complex systems involving enormous numbers, mathematical notation, and fast-growing functions.

Her work developed from early experiments with fictional large numbers into increasingly structured systems of her own. She continues to study existing notation systems while developing original ideas and mathematical constructions.

Her first creation in fictional googology was Aen's Number, marking the beginning of her work with fictional large-number systems.

Fictional Googology

Rebecca's work in fictional googology is separate from her more formally structured mathematical notation projects. It provides a space for creating original large-number concepts, systems, names, and structures that can be explored creatively without being limited to established mathematical frameworks.

Aen's Number was the first major creation in this area. It marked the beginning of Rebecca's own fictional large-number projects and later inspired further experimentation with increasingly elaborate systems.

Personal Life

Rebecca is Catholic and considers her faith an important part of her personal values. She was previously atheist, later became Christian, and eventually became Catholic, which is her current religious affiliation.

She also holds pacifist views and does not support wars, preferring peaceful solutions to conflicts.
